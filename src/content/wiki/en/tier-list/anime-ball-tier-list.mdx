1. **Guko Ultra Instinct**: Features a passive autonomous drift phase. When rival balls initiate a high-speed rush, Guko slides past direct frontal impact and strikes from behind with enhanced counter-velocity.
2. **Jinwu Shadow Monarch**: Deploys shadow clones upon heavy arena collisions, turning 1v1 matches into chaotic 2v1 pressure situations.
