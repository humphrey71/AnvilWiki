The Halloween update has landed in Roblox's Anime Ball Duels, and it is the biggest seasonal event the game has seen: a **haunted island** that rises next to the lobby every hour from :00 to :30, **Candy Corn** as a brand-new event currency, nightly **Boss Raids** against three limited monsters, and a wave of new fighters headlined by **Nezoko**, **Kaniko Kakuja** and the limited trio **Mahyto, Alukard and Muzon**. The event runs **until November 3**.

![Anime Ball Duels Halloween Update](/images/hero.webp)

Before grinding the event, make sure you redeem both active Part 2 drops — `WEAREBACK` and `HACKERSLOST` — worth a combined 22,500 Coins, 1,500 Gems, 75 Trait Crystals, 15 Spins and **3,000 Candy Corn** from our verified [Anime Ball Duels Codes Directory](/codes/all-codes/).

## Halloween Part 2: October 29 — Nightmare Raids & Three New Limited Bosses

Part 2 goes live on **October 29 at 17:00 UTC (October 30, 1:00 AM Beijing time)** and it reshapes the raid meta:

* **3 new raid bosses**: **Grimjo**, **Kenjako** and **Neferpito** — three brand-new **Limited** anime fighters.
* **Nightmare Raids**: any raid has a **5% chance** to turn into a Nightmare — a harder boss with a **5x drop chance**.
* **Boss of the Day** — one of the three bosses every day: **1.5x Candy Corn** and **extra pity** on that boss.
* **Global kill goal**: every **300 bosses beaten across ALL servers** grants **2X Luck for everyone for 30 minutes**, and Nightmare Bosses count as **3 kills**.
* **New Halloween missions** plus **boss hunter titles**.

> ⚠️ **Last chance warning**: **Mahyto, Alukard and Muzon become unobtainable** once Part 2 lands — clear the Limited Capsule or farm their raids before the update goes live.

**Trading arrives on October 31 at 17:00 UTC (November 1, 1:00 AM Beijing time)**: trade your anime fighters, explosions and gems with other players — Shiny status, traits and serial numbers all travel with the trade.

## 1. Candy Corn: How to Farm the Event Currency

Candy Corn powers everything this event — the Halloween gacha, the Candy Shop and the limited capsule grind. Every source in the update:

* **Duel wins** — Halloween maps grant **1.5x candy**, so queue duels on the event maps first.
* **Boss Raids** — the single biggest payout: **150 Candy Corn per win** (50 on a loss, MVP bonus on top).
* **Missions** — the Halloween mission line also pays out Candy Corn and the **Pumpkin King title**.
* **AFK rewards** — the **daily AFK limit is gone**, and AFK drops Candy Corn for the whole event. Idle farming is back on the menu.
* **Island pickup** — candy spawns lying around the haunted island. Grab it while the night phase lasts (every hour from :00 to :30).

> **Watch out for the pumpkins**: they chase you down and explode. The knockback is not worth the candy — dodge them unless you are tanky.

To double your income windows, the Candy Shop sells **Candy Rush** (2x candy for 30 minutes) — pop one before a raid session and stack it with the 1.5x map bonus.

## 2. Halloween Raid: Boss Rotation, Tickets & Rewards

The **Halloween Raid** is the centerpiece PvE mode:

* Up to **4 players** fight the night's boss: **Mahyto**, **Alukard** or **Muzon** — the boss **changes every night**.
* Bosses fight in **phases**, summon **minions**, and unleash an **ult at 20% HP**. You can **revive once** per raid.
* A raid costs **1 Boss Ticket**. You get **2 free tickets per day** (stacking to a max of 5), and the Candy Shop sells more.
* **Win = 150 Candy Corn**, loss = 50, and the MVP earns a bonus. Wins have a **10% chance to refund your ticket**.
* The boss has a **1% chance to drop as a fighter** — the only way to earn Mahyto, Alukard or Muzon without the capsule.
* **Join Raid** now finds raids on **other servers** too, so you are never stuck waiting in an empty lobby.

Bring your best S-Tier collision fighter into raids — the [Anime Ball Tier List](/tier-list/anime-ball-tier-list/) covers the strongest picks.

## 3. Every New Halloween Fighter

**Halloween gacha (Candy Corn pulls):**

* **Nezoko** — Mythic
* **Togo** — Godly. In 2v2 and raids, Togo now **copies a teammate** (never the boss), making her a unique support pick for duos.
* **Hysoka** — Secret
* **Dooma** — Void

**Limited fighters (capsule & raid drops):**

* **Mahyto**, **Alukard** and **Muzon** — 0.5% each per Limited Capsule, guaranteed by your 100th capsule, or a 1% drop from their own raid.
* ⚠️ **Unobtainable after October 29**: Halloween Part 2 removes the limited trio from the capsule and raid rotation — your last chance is before the update lands (17:00 UTC).

**Robux exclusive:**

* **Kaniko Kakuja** — 1,599 Robux bundled with the **Kakuja explosion**, or 1,299 Robux for just the fighter. Test him on the dummy at his stand before you buy.

## 4. Limited Capsule, Candy Shop & VIP

* **LIMITED CAPSULE** — only Halloween fighters. Pricing: x1 for 99, x3 for 279, x5 for 449, and x10 for 849 **with +1 free capsule**. Each limited fighter is 0.5% per capsule; a limited is **guaranteed by your 100th capsule**.
* **Candy Shop** — Candy Rush (2x candy, 30 min), Shiny Brew (2x shiny, 15 min), Vampire Brew, extra Boss Tickets, **5 Halloween dances** and **4 titles**.
* **VIP** — +50% luck, +25% coins, +25% gems and a rainbow name. VIP can be **gifted** to friends.

## 5. Quality-of-Life Changes in the Halloween Update

* **New leaderboards**: Best Win Streak and Top Boss Raid Wins, tracked on the island.
* **Titles tab** in the inventory — wear any title you own, including Pumpkin King from the Halloween missions.
* **Potions tab** in the inventory for faster buff management.
* **No more daily AFK limit**, with Candy Corn flowing from AFK during the event.
* Prettier capsule animations for the Halloween fighters, plus fixes for raids getting stuck when a player left and candy you could not pick up after a fight.

## 6. Best Way to Spend Your Halloween Budget

1. **Redeem both codes first** — 3,000 free Candy Corn before you grind a single raid.
