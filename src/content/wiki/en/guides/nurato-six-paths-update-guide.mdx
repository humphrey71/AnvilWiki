## 1. New Divine Fighter: Nurato Six Paths

As the third official Divine fighter in Anime Ball Duels, Nurato Six Paths introduces **Six Paths Sage Mode**, wielding three devastating signature combat mechanics:

| Ability | Core Mechanism | Arena Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| **Truth-Seeking Orbs** | Six floating black orbs orbit behind him | Orbs automatically lock on and track down retreating opponents |
| **Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken** | High-velocity wind-blade collision sphere | Traps rival balls in an expanding vortex; damage & knockback scale the longer enemies remain inside |
| **Baryon Mode** | Awakens Kurama aura & ignites fiery orbs | Converts orbital attacks into continuous burn damage and **steals opponent HP** on contact |

Because Baryon Mode actively drains enemy vitality while restoring your own ball's resilience, Nurato Six Paths instantly commands the absolute pinnacle of our [Anime Ball Tier List](/tier-list/anime-ball-tier-list/).

## 2. Overhauled Gacha Pity System (Guaranteed Pulls)

Tired of bad roll luck? Update 1.5.1 adds **visible Pity Counters** directly on the summon UI, ensuring transparent milestones across all banner tiers:

* **Elite Banner**: Guaranteed **Mythic+** within **50 rolls**.
* **Limited Banner**: Guaranteed **Godly+** within **40 rolls** (Optimal for pulling Nurato Six Paths).
* **Premium Gacha**: Guaranteed **Secret+** within **40 rolls**.

Both active counters are now displayed in real-time above the roll button, making it easier to plan Gem expenditures.

## 3. Cross-Server Matchmaking & 2v2 Party Tab

Queuing solo on low-population servers is now a thing of the past thanks to global matchmaking improvements:
1. **Cross-Server Quick Play & 2v2**: The matchmaker now automatically queries active lobbies across all global Roblox servers, drastically reducing queue times.
2. **New Party Tab**: You can now invite friends directly into a pre-made team to queue into 2v2 arenas together. To coordinate team compositions, join fellow duelists in our [Official Discord Community Guide](/guides/discord-server-link/).
3. **4-Player 2v2 Map Vote**: Before every 2v2 match, all four players vote between competing arena hazards, with the majority selection deciding the ring.
