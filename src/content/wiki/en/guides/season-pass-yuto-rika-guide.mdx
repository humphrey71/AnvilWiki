Introduced in [Anime Ball Duels Update 1.5](/guides/update-1-5-patch-notes/), the **Season Pass** represents the game's flagship progression system. Spanning 50 distinct reward milestones, reaching the final tier awards one of the most powerful limited fighters in game history: **Yuto with Rika**.

![Anime Ball Duels Season Pass Yuto Clash](/images/hero.webp)

Whether you are looking to unlock custom Phonk dances or aiming straight for the Tier 50 capstone, here is your definitive guide to completing the pass efficiently.

## How the Season Pass Progression Works

The Season Pass rewards active participation across all game modes:
* **Match Completions**: Every 1v1 and 2v2 arena clash earns base Pass XP (with bonuses awarded for match victories).
* **Tier Skips via Codes**: You can instantly skip a tier by redeeming the active promo code `BATTLEPASS` from our [Anime Ball Duels Codes Directory](/codes/all-codes/).
* **Daily Quests**: Rotating objectives (such as executing 10 ring-outs or winning 3 consecutive matches) provide large chunks of bonus experience.

| Milestone | Key Rewards | Gameplay Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 - 10** | Coins, Gems & Starter Crystals | Funds early rolls on standard banners |
| **Tier 15 & 30** | Exclusive Phonk Victory Dances | Custom celebration animations on round wins |
| **Tier 25 & 40** | High-grade Trait Crystals | Reroll combat traits in the [Character Mastery](/guides/update-1-5-patch-notes/) tab |
| **Tier 50 (Final)** | **Yuto with Rika (Limited)** | S+ God-Tier dual fighter with spirit shield & countercleave |

## Why Yuto with Rika Dominates the Meta

Ranked at the very top of our [Anime Ball Tier List](/tier-list/anime-ball-tier-list/), Yuto with Rika operates as a **dual-entity fighter**:

1. **Rika's Protective Barrier**: Upon entering collision clashes, Rika manifests as a spectral aura, absorbing first-hit momentum similarly to Gojo's infinity.
2. **Love Blast Counter-Thrust**: When knocked toward the boundary hazards, Yuto channels a reverse shockwave that blasts colliding rivals into the opposite wall.
3. **Synergy with Other Fighters**: In 2v2 team matches, Yuto pairs seamlessly with heavy anchors like [Rengoko from the AFK World](/guides/rengoko-afk-world-guide/) to control the ring perimeter.

## 3 Tips to Finish All 50 Tiers Before the Season Ends

### 1. Maximize 2v2 Arena Queues
While 1v1 matches are fast, 2v2 battles offer higher XP multipliers per minute of active combat. Teaming up with a partner via voice channels in the [Official Discord Server](/guides/discord-server-link/) ensures consistent win streaks.

### 2. Level Character Mastery Simultaneously
While farming Season Pass tiers, your active balls continuously gain familiarity toward their 10 mastery levels. Prioritize using fighters with high mass and rebound velocity to climb both systems together.

### 3. Track Remaining Event Days
Yuto with Rika is a **strictly limited reward** tied to this season. If you fall behind schedule, save earned Gems from duel victories to purchase tier skips ahead of the final seasonal reset.
