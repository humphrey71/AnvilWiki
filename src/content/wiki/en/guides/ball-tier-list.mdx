## How are balls evaluated in competitive PvP?

Each ball is rated against four standardized performance benchmarks rather than cosmetic rarity alone:

1. **Raw Damage Output**: Total health depleted across a standard round duration.
2. **Hit Consistency**: How reliably the ball connects its special mechanics regardless of arena size.
3. **Counter Resilience**: Whether the ball has severe vulnerabilities to common meta counters.
4. **Utility & CC**: The presence of crowd control, slowing effects, or defensive sustain.
