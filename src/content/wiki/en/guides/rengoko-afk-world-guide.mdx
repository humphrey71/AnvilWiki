With the arrival of [Anime Ball Duels Update 1.5](/guides/update-1-5-patch-notes/), developer King Games TD introduced the brand-new **AFK World lobby**. Among its passive rewards is one of the most coveted fighters in the game: **Rengoko (Flame Breathing)**, dropping at a rare **0.5% chance exclusively inside the AFK World**.

![Anime Ball Duels Rengoko Clash](/images/hero.webp)

If you are hunting for this top-tier flame powerhouse, here is the complete breakdown of how the AFK World works and how to optimize your farming time.

## How to Enter the AFK World in Anime Ball Duels

Accessing the AFK World requires no currency or level unlock:
1. Launch **Anime Ball Duels** on Roblox.
2. In the main hub plaza, locate the purple glowing portal labeled **AFK World**.
3. Walk into the portal to teleport your character to the dedicated AFK zone.
4. While staying in this zone, the game automatically awards Coins, Gems, and roll chances every few minutes without requiring manual input.

Before entering your AFK session, ensure you have redeemed working rewards from our [Anime Ball Duels Codes Directory](/codes/all-codes/) to maximize your currency bank.

## Understanding the 0.5% Drop Rate

Unlike conventional Gacha banners where you spend Coins or Gems, Rengoko drops through **passive roll cycles**:

| Metric | Stat Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Drop Rate** | 0.5% (1 in 200 cycles) | Exclusive to AFK World |
| **Roll Interval** | ~60 to 90 seconds | Automated cycle per timer tick |
| **Additional Rewards** | Coins, Gems, Trait Crystals | Earned simultaneously every cycle |
| **Tier Ranking** | S-Tier | Flame trail & perimeter knockback |

Because Rengoko cannot be purchased with Robux or rolled from the standard 50+ character pool, setting up an AFK farming session is the sole method to add him to your collection.

## Why Rengoko Is Dominant in 1v1 and 2v2 Arenas

Rengoko is ranked as a premier pick on our [Anime Ball Tier List](/tier-list/anime-ball-tier-list/) for several key reasons:

1. **Flame Trail Hazard**: As Rengoko rolls across the floor, he leaves a scorched trail that decelerates opposing balls upon contact.
2. **Purgatory Smash Knockback**: On high-speed head-on collisions, Rengoko releases a burst shockwave that propels opponents directly into ring-out barriers.
3. **Mastery Mass Scaling**: Leveling Rengoko through the new [Character Mastery system](/guides/update-1-5-patch-notes/) grants substantial momentum retention on boundary bounces.

## Pro Tips for AFK Farming

* **Use an Auto-Clicker or Anti-Idle Tool**: Roblox automatically disconnects inactive players after 20 minutes. Keep a periodic click or jump active to prevent disconnections.
* **Combine with Season Pass Grinding**: Complete your daily active PvP matches to farm [Season Pass XP](/guides/season-pass-yuto-rika-guide/) first, then leave your client running in the AFK World overnight.
* **Community News**: If King Games TD activates limited double-luck AFK events, notifications drop first in their server. Check out our [Official Discord Community Guide](/guides/discord-server-link/) to stay informed!
