Every sphere in Roblox Ball vs Ball belongs to one of three overarching families: **Ball**, **Explosion**, or **Flyer**. Mastering how these three archetypes interact is the foundation of drafting winning rounds during the 1v1 selection phase.

## What are the differences between the three Ball Families?

The primary differences lie in trajectory physics, impact triggers, and hazard immunity. Standard Balls maximize direct collision frequency and sustained effects; Explosion balls detonate radius blasts on contact; Flyers bypass ground-level hazards by utilizing lofted parabolic flight arcs.

## How do family matchups counter each other in the arena?

Understanding the tactical counter triangle gives you an immediate drafting edge when choosing from your three round options:

- **Explosion counters Standard Ball**: High-blast detonations knock standard balls out of their optimal bounce paths, canceling their combo momentum.
- **Flyer counters Explosion**: Because Explosion spheres rely on arena surface impacts, Flyers elevate above blast radiuses and strike from blind spots.
- **Standard Ball counters Flyer**: Dense collision stats on heavy Standard balls swat erratic Flyers out of the air when launch trajectories intersect.
