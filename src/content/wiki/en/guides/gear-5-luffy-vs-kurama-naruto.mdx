## Gear 5 Luffy: The Elastic Momentum Sweeper

Gear 5 Luffy leverages a unique **Rubber Physics passive**. When deflected toward the arena perimeter, Luffy retains over 80% of his forward velocity and gains instant trajectory inversion. This allows you to turn high-speed enemy smashes into lightning-fast counter-bounces.

* **Strengths**: Maximum speed scaling, virtually immune to single-hit corner ring-outs.
* **Weaknesses**: Can overshoot arena borders on open hazards if ricochet angles misalign.

## Kurama Naruto: The Heavy Chakra Juggernaut

In contrast to Luffy's bouncy agility, Kurama Naruto relies on raw **Collision Mass and Shockwave Burst**. With superior weight scaling, Naruto resists heavy knockback from rival balls and unleashes continuous chakra flares that destabilize cluster fights in 2v2 arenas.

* **Strengths**: Anchors firmly in the center circle; pushes lighter balls off the platform effortlessly.
* **Weaknesses**: Slower initial roll acceleration during early round seconds.

## Head-to-Head: Who Wins the 1v1 Clash?

In a direct 1v1 showdown:
1. **At Mid-Range Collisions**: Kurama Naruto holds the advantage due to raw weight and fire cleave damage.
2. **On Edge Rebounds**: Gear 5 Luffy dominates with rubber bounce acceleration, counter-launching opponents into ring-outs.
