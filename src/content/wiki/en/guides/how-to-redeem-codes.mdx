## How do you enter and activate promo codes in Ball vs Ball?

To enter promo codes in Ball vs Ball, launch the experience, tap the gift box icon in the top-left section of your screen, paste a valid code into the text prompt, and tap the green Redeem button. Your Coins will be credited to your balance instantly.

## What are the most common code redemption errors and fixes?

When a redemption code fails to grant coins, review the following troubleshooting checklist before reporting a bug to developer ATYS 3:

1. **Trailing Whitespace**: Copying codes from websites often copies an invisible space at the end. Delete extra spaces before submitting.
2. **Case Sensitivity**: While most codes like `upd5` are lowercase, certain platform updates require exact matching.
3. **One-Time Use Limit**: Codes in Ball vs Ball can only be redeemed once per Roblox user ID.
4. **Group Verification**: Certain promotional gifts require players to join the official ATYS 3 Roblox community group.
