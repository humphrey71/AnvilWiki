Roblox Ball vs Ball combines real-time trajectory aiming with tactical collection building. Understanding the match structure, resource economy, and ball synergies early on allows you to climb win streaks and acquire competitive high-tier spheres.

## How do you play and win matches in Ball vs Ball?

To win matches in Ball vs Ball, you must deplete your opponent's three health hearts before they deplete yours. At the beginning of each round, the game presents you with three random balls from your inventory; you choose one, set your trajectory toward the enemy, and let physics handle the combat.

## What are the best strategies for managing Gacha and Rerolls?

Smart resource management separates novice players from arena veterans. Never enter battles relying on the default Starter Ball; instead, invest every hundred coins into the Gacha machine until you unlock versatile powerhouses ranked in our [Ball Tier List](/guides/ball-tier-list/).

1. **Prioritize Collection Width**: Because the selection phase draws randomly from your pool, owning more unique balls directly increases the probability of drafting counter-picks.
2. **Save Gems for High-Stakes Rounds**: Do not spend Gems rerolling in Round 1. Save reroll currency for Round 3 when both players are down to their final heart.
3. **Learn Ball Family Matchups**: Study the distinct physics profiles explained in our [Ball Families Guide](/guides/ball-families-guide/) to counter heavy jumpers with high-flying projectiles.
4. **Master Wall Traps**: Launching balls like [Laser Ball](/balls/laser-ball/) into corners forces enemy spheres through persistent hazard grids.
