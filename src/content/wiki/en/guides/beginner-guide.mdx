Anime Ball Duels combines fast-paced physics-based rolling combat with iconic anime powerhouses on Roblox. Developed by King Games TD, the game features competitive 1v1 and 2v2 auto-battle arenas where surviving as the last ball standing earns you Coins, Gems, and rare Crystals.

## How do round drafting and auto-battle mechanics work?

At the start of every duel round, you are prompted to **choose 1 of 3 random Anime Balls** drafted from your unlocked Gacha roster. After making your selection, balls enter the arena and engage in physics-driven auto-battle combat. Collisions build momentum, knock back rivals, and trigger unique anime skills.
