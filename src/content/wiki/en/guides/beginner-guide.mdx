Anime Ball Duels pits fighters against each other in fast-paced round-based elimination battles on Roblox. Each player controls an anime-themed ball equipped with unique traits and special abilities. Surviving until the end of the round grants coins, trophies, and leaderboard ranking.

## How does the core combat system work in Anime Ball Duels?

Combat in Anime Ball Duels revolves around ball trajectory control, momentum preservation, and timing defensive deflects. When a round begins, the game randomly assigns you one of your unlocked Anime Balls. Knocking opposing players out of the arena or depleting their health bar eliminates them from the duel.

## How do you use the mid-battle reroll mechanic effectively?

The mid-battle reroll is Anime Ball Duels' signature competitive feature. If you draft a close-range character against a heavy projectile zoner, trigger the reroll option from your HUD to swap into a different Anime Ball. Do not waste rerolls in rounds where you already hold positional or elemental advantage.
