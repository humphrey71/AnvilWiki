## What new features are coming in Anime Ball Duels Update 1.5?

Update 1.5 delivers a major expansion to both character collection and competitive arena dueling. According to the official developer patch brief, players can expect the following additions:

![Anime Ball Duels Update 1.5 Clash](/images/articles/deku-vs-tanjiro.webp)

* **New Limited Gacha**: Exclusive, time-limited anime fighters with signature abilities available only during the patch event. Pull them before the banner rotates out!
* **New Anime Fighters**: Permanent additions to the standard Gacha pool, bringing unique collision passives and active abilities.
* **New Arenas**: Fresh battle maps featuring distinct boundary bounce mechanics, hazards, and ring-out layouts.
* **Big Balance Update**: Retuned fighter collision weight, deflect timing, and skill cooldowns for fairer, more competitive 1v1 and 2v2 duels.
* **New Visual Effects**: Flashier impact animations, trail glows, and upgraded skill bursts during high-velocity collisions.
