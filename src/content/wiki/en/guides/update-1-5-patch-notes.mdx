The much-anticipated **Anime Ball Duels Update 1.5** has officially arrived on Roblox! Delivered by King Games TD, this massive content update introduces the game's first-ever **Season Pass**, a deep **Character Mastery system**, the time-limited **Tozi Capsule**, and five god-tier anime duelists.

## Overview of Key Additions in Update 1.5

| Feature | Details | Key Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Season Pass** | 50 reward tiers of Coins, Gems & Phonk dances | Unlocks limited **Yuto with Rika** at Tier 50 |
| **Character Mastery** | 10 progressive mastery levels per fighter | Permanent collision mass, bounce agility & stat boosts |
| **Tozi Capsule** | Exclusive 7-day limited banner | High chance to summon the Heavenly Restriction assassin **Tozi** |
| **New Anime Balls** | Guko Ultra Instinct, Jinwu, Naoyo, Rengoko | Metagame-defining abilities and collision traits |
| **AFK World Lobby** | Passive progression reward room | 0.5% drop chance for the legendary fire ball **Rengoko** |
| **Arena Mechanics** | Reworked physics & dynamic round map draws | Fairer 1v1 collisions and randomized map hazards |

## 1. Season Pass & Limited Fighter: Yuto with Rika

Update 1.5 introduces the inaugural 50-tier **Season Pass**. Players earn Pass XP by competing in daily 1v1 and 2v2 duel matches, finishing daily missions, and redeeming milestone codes like `BATTLEPASS`.

* **Tier 50 Capstone**: Grants the exclusive, time-limited **Yuto with Rika** ball, pairing unbreakable barrier shields with high-mass countercleaves.
* **Bonus Cosmetics**: Leveling through the pass also unlocks exclusive **Phonk victory dances** and rare Trait Crystals.
* Read our complete walkthrough in the [Season Pass & Yuto Unlock Guide](/guides/season-pass-yuto-rika-guide/).

## 2. Character Mastery (10 Levels per Fighter)

Instead of relying solely on gacha dupes, Update 1.5 allows you to deepen each fighter's individual potential through **Character Mastery**:
* Every anime ball now features **10 mastery levels**.
* Playing ranked duels builds familiarity points, increasing collision weight, edge rebound velocity, and active skill proc odds.
* Redeem the new code `MASTERY` in-game for 20 free Trait Crystals to jumpstart your upgrades.

## 3. Limited Tozi Capsule (7 Days Only)

For a limited 7-day window, players can roll on the **Tozi Capsule** banner. Tozi features a specialized physical piercing trait that bypasses energy shields—making him an exceptional hard-counter against Gojo's infinity barrier.

## 4. Brand New Anime Fighters

Update 1.5 expands the roster with four highly sought-after characters:

1. **Guko Ultra Instinct**: High-agility duelist with autonomous dodge-phase collision physics.
2. **Jinwu Shadow Monarch**: Summons shadowy collision deflections that dominate 2v2 cross-fire battles.
3. **Naoyo**: Frame-rate projection acceleration that speeds up across successive wall rebounds.
4. **Rengoko (Flame Breathing)**: High-temperature shockwaves with devastating perimeter knockouts. Rengoko drops at a **0.5% rate exclusively in the AFK World**. Read our dedicated [Rengoko AFK World Guide](/guides/rengoko-afk-world-guide/) to learn how to farm him efficiently.

## 5. Map Draw & Arena Reworks

To ensure competitive fairness in 1v1 and 2v2 arenas, King Games TD has introduced a **randomized Map Draw system before every round**. Arenas now feature distinct boundary elasticity and center hazards, preventing players from relying on a single static rebound angle.
