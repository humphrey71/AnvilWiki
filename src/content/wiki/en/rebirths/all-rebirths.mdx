## How does the rebirth system work in Ride A Pet?

Rebirth is the core progression loop: trade your current cash and unhatched eggs for a permanent multiplier boost and extra pet slots, then re-climb with better earning power. Hatched pets stay with you, so each rebirth makes the next climb faster. Seven tiers exist, each gated by cash plus one specific pet.

## What do you keep vs. lose on rebirth?

| Kept | Reset |
| --- | --- |
| All hatched pets | Cash on hand |
| Pet slots from prior rebirths | Unopened eggs |
| Permanent multipliers | — |

Because required pets are consumed check-list style, never sell your last [Horse, Fox, Unicorn, Phoenix, Kitsune, or Dragon](/pets/pets-tier-list/) — the moment you can afford the cash cost, the pet is the only blocker.

## What is the fastest path to each rebirth?

1. **Rebirth 1–2**: farm starter [eggs](/eggs/all-eggs/) for Horses and Foxes while stacking money — these two multipliers pay for everything after.
2. **Rebirth 3**: Unicorn is a rare hatch, so volume-hatch mid-tier eggs; income from penned Phoenix-type pets funds the cash cost.
3. **Rebirth 4–7**: Ethereal territory. Check the [pet tier list](/pets/pets-tier-list/) for hatch odds, and don't miss weather windows — see [mutations](/mutations/all-mutations/).
