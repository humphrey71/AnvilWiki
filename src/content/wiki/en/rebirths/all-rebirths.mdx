## How does the rebirth system work in Ride A Pet?

Rebirth is the core progression loop: you trade your current cash and unhatched eggs for a permanent multiplier boost and extra pet slots, then re-climb with better earning power. Pets you've already hatched stay with you, so each rebirth makes the next climb faster. Seven rebirth tiers exist, each gated by cash plus one specific pet.

## What are all 7 rebirth requirements?

| Rebirth | Money cost | Required pet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | $1M | Horse |
| 2 | $500M | Fox |
| 3 | $2.5B | Unicorn |
| 4 | $125B+ | Phoenix |
| 5 | not yet confirmed | Kitsune |
| 6 | not yet confirmed | Kitsune |
| 7 | not yet confirmed | Dragon |

<Callout type="warn">Costs for Rebirths 5–7 are not yet published with verified numbers — guides disagree on the exact figures. Treat any specific number you see elsewhere as an estimate until verified in-game; this table locks in as data is confirmed.</Callout>

## What do you keep vs. lose on rebirth?

| Kept | Reset |
| --- | --- |
| All hatched pets | Cash on hand |
| Pet slots from prior rebirths | Unopened eggs |
| Permanent multipliers | — |

Because required pets are consumed check-list style, never sell your last [Horse, Fox, Unicorn, Phoenix, Kitsune, or Dragon](/pets/pets-tier-list/) — the moment you can afford the cash cost, the pet is the only blocker.

## What is the fastest path to each rebirth?

1. **Rebirth 1–2**: farm [starter eggs](/eggs/) for Horses and Foxes while stacking money — the multipliers from these two pay for everything after.
2. **Rebirth 3**: Unicorn is a 1-in-1T hatch, so volume-hatch mid-tier eggs; income from penned Phoenix-type pets funds the $2.5B.
3. **Rebirth 4–7**: you're in Ethereal territory. Check the [pet tier list](/pets/pets-tier-list/) for hatch odds and prioritize weather events — a mutated required pet is both the payment and the trophy.

<Callout type="tip">Before confirming any rebirth, check the in-game panel to verify you meet the live requirements — and if a weather event is running, let your required pet catch a mutation first (see [mutations](/mutations/all-mutations/)).</Callout>
