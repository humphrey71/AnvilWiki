## What are mutations in Ride A Pet?

Mutations are permanent multipliers applied to a pet's speed, money, earnings, and selling price. There are 8 in total: 5 come from weather events and 3 roll when an egg hatches. The two types stack — the core of late-game value, and the reason a mutated mid-tier pet can outsell a plain top-tier one.

## How do weather events work?

Weather events are the main mutation engine: each runs 5 minutes, all five types trigger with equal chance, and while one is live **every egg on the map** mutates. Eggs you collect during the window hatch as mutated pets — the mutation is locked to the egg, not the timer.

The practical loop: sprint the map, grab every egg you can reach, and prioritize rare eggs over common ones (the mutation multiplies an already-valuable base — hatch odds per pet are in the [pet tier list](/pets/pets-tier-list/)).

## Can hatch mutations and weather mutations stack?

Yes — this is where the top-end pets come from. A pet that hatches Gold and then catches a Void event carries both multipliers. Drop odds for double mutations are not published anywhere; treat any specific percentage you see as a community estimate.

## Which mutation should you chase first?

For early progression, **Rage (4x)** offers the best effort-to-value ratio. Save the Eternal chase for when you can cross the map fast — a 5-minute window only pays off if you can grab many eggs in it. Before any rebirth, check whether a weather event is live; mutating your rebirth-bound pets first is a known efficiency trick — the [rebirth guide](/rebirths/all-rebirths/) lists every required pet.

## What is the fastest route to a mutated dream pet?

1. Watch for a weather event (all five types, equal chance).
2. During the 5-minute window, grab rare eggs only.
3. Hatch immediately — the mutation is locked to the egg.
4. Roll hatch mutations by volume hatching.
5. Combine: hold hatched mutants and ride them through the next weather event.
