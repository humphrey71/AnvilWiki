## What are mutations in Ride A Pet?

Mutations are permanent multipliers applied to a pet's speed, money, earnings, and selling price. There are 8 in total: 5 come from weather events and 3 roll when an egg hatches. The two types stack, so an ideally-mutated pet can carry both a hatch mutation and a weather mutation — the core of late-game value in this game.

## How do weather events work?

Weather events are the main mutation engine. Every 5-minute event mutates **every egg on the map**, and any eggs you collect during the window hatch as mutated pets (a 100% inheritance — a Shocked Egg always hatches a Shocked pet). Pets you're riding or penned also have a chance to mutate during weather, but the exact probability is unknown.

The practical loop during an event: sprint the map, grab every egg you can reach, and let the mutation apply for free. Prioritize rare eggs over common ones — the mutation multiplies an already-valuable base.

## Can hatch mutations and weather mutations stack?

Yes — this is where the top-end pets come from. A pet that hatches Gold (hatch mutation) and then catches a Void event (weather mutation) carries both multipliers. Community consensus ranks the strongest combos as Rainbow + Eternal and Gold + Void on top-tier pets like Dragon or Phoenix, but drop odds for double mutations are not published anywhere — treat any specific percentage you see as community estimate.

## Which mutation should you chase first?

For early progression, **Rage (4x)** offers the best effort-to-value ratio because mid-rarity weather events are frequent enough to farm. Save the Eternal chase for when you can cross the map fast — a 5-minute window only pays off if you can grab many eggs in it. Before any rebirth, check whether a weather event is live; mutating your rebirth-bound pets first is a common efficiency trick.

## What is the fastest route to a mutated dream pet?

1. Watch for a weather event (all five types trigger with equal chance).
2. During the 5-minute window, grab rare eggs only.
3. Hatch immediately — the mutation is locked to the egg, not the timer.
4. Roll hatch mutations (Gold/Diamond/Rainbow) by volume hatching.
5. Combine: hold hatched mutants and ride them through the next weather event.
