## How does egg spawning work?

Eggs spawn at fixed locations and **reset every 8 minutes**. Community-reported zones:

| Zone | What spawns there |
| --- | --- |
| Near spawn area | Black Hole Eggs — spawn-friendly for beginners (see the [locations guide](/eggs/black-hole-egg-locations/)) |
| Waterfall, caves, mountains | Mid-tier eggs on rotation |
| Pyramid, giant structure | Higher-rarity spawns |

Two ways to find eggs: memorize spawn points, or buy a **radar** from the gears shop to cover more spawns per 8-minute window. Weather events mutate every egg on the map while they last — the [mutations guide](/mutations/all-mutations/) has the full multiplier table.

## Which eggs should you hunt first?

- **Early (pre-Rebirth 1)**: common and rare eggs fill your nests — volume hatching beats rarity when you need a [Horse](/pets/pets-tier-list/)
- **Mid (Rebirth 2-3)**: Epic/Legendary eggs for speed-tier pets; a radar pays for itself here
- **Endgame**: Divine and Ethereal eggs, ideally during a weather event
