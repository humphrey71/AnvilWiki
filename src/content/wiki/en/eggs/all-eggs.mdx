## How does egg spawning work?

Eggs spawn at fixed locations around the map and **reset every 8 minutes**. Community findings on spawn zones:

| Zone | What spawns there |
| --- | --- |
| Near spawn area | Black Hole Eggs (best beginner farming spot — see our [Black Hole Egg locations guide](/eggs/black-hole-egg-locations/)) |
| Waterfall, caves, mountains | Mid-tier eggs on rotation |
| Pyramid, giant structure | Higher-rarity spawns |

The two ways to find eggs: memorize spawn points, or buy a **radar** from the gears shop, which points you to the nearest eggs so a single 8-minute window covers more spawns.

## Which eggs should you hunt first?

- **Early (pre-Rebirth 1)**: common and rare eggs fill your nests — volume hatching beats rarity when you need a [Horse](/pets/pets-tier-list/).
- **Mid (Rebirth 2-3)**: Epic/Legendary eggs for speed-tier pets; a radar pays for itself here.
- **Endgame**: Divine and Ethereal eggs. Route between the few known spawns and be on the map when a weather event hits — a mutated Black Hole Egg is a trophy hatch.

<Callout type="tip">Weather events mutate every egg on the map for 5 minutes. The best egg run of your session is during one: same 8-minute spawns, but every hatch comes out mutated (see [mutations](/mutations/all-mutations/)).</Callout>
