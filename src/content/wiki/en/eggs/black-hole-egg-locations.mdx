## Where does the Black Hole Egg spawn?

Black Hole Eggs spawn **near the spawn area** — a beginner-friendly exception to the usual rule. Most egg types cluster near the shops; Black Hole Eggs do the opposite. That quirk matters for routing: your first loop of every session should sweep the spawn area before heading to the shop cluster.

The egg itself is **Ethereal tier at 1-in-100B luck**, the third-rarest in the game behind [Solaris and Cherub](/eggs/all-eggs/). Farmable endgame rarity in a beginner-accessible zone is why this egg anchors most mid-game progression plans.

## What is the best Black Hole farming loop?

1. Start at spawn, sweep the immediate area for Black Hole spawns.
2. Route outward toward waterfall/caves (mid-tier eggs on the way).
3. Return to spawn before the 8-minute reset to catch the fresh wave.
4. Upgrade with a radar from the gears shop once income allows.

<Callout type="tip">Pair the loop with a fast mount — every extra spawn you hit per 8-minute window compounds. The [pet tier list](/pets/pets-tier-list/) ranks the best rides at each stage.</Callout>

## Why hunt Black Hole Eggs during weather events?

Weather events mutate every egg on the map for 5 minutes. A Black Hole Egg collected during an Eternal event hatches as a 100x-mutated pet — the [mutations guide](/mutations/all-mutations/) has the full multiplier table. Because mutated eggs inherit their mutation 100% of the time, weather windows turn your farming loop into a mutation lottery with guaranteed payouts.

## What comes after Black Hole Eggs?

The full [egg table](/eggs/all-eggs/) lists Solaris (1 in 300B) and Cherub (1 in 1T) above it. Both spawn in rarer zones, so most players sequence: Black Hole loop for income → Divine eggs for speed-tier pets → Solaris/Cherub only with radar and a maxed mount. Meanwhile, the [rebirth guide](/rebirths/all-rebirths/) tells you which pets to hold back for each rebirth tier.
