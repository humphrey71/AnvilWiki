## What is the best pet in Ride A Pet?

Kitsune and Dragon are the two S-tier pets. Kitsune is the confirmed fastest mount in the game at roughly 1 billion speed. Dragon is an endgame hatch — community data suggests it is actually the harder pull of the two, though its egg source is not yet verified (see the note under the stats table).

For how mutations multiply a pet's stats, see the [mutations guide](/mutations/all-mutations/).

## How do pets connect to rebirths?

All seven rebirths are gated behind specific pets: Horse → Fox → Unicorn → Phoenix → Kitsune → Kitsune → Dragon. Never sell your last Horse or Fox even after they look obsolete — the [rebirth requirements guide](/rebirths/all-rebirths/) has the full costs alongside.

## Which pets should you ride vs. sell?

- **Ride**: your fastest pet (speed = more eggs per minute), ideally weather-mutated — multipliers in the [mutations guide](/mutations/all-mutations/)
- **Pen**: high income/s earners, especially Phoenix-type pets
- **Never sell**: one of each rebirth-required pet

<Callout type="tip">During any weather event, grab rare eggs only — a mutated Phoenix beats ten plain ones. Egg spawn zones are in the [egg guide](/eggs/all-eggs/).</Callout>
