## What is the best pet in Ride A Pet?

Kitsune and Dragon are the two S-tier pets. Kitsune is the confirmed fastest mount in the game at roughly 1 billion speed, with a hatch chance around 1 in 1 Qi (quintillion). Dragon hatches exclusively from the Dragon Egg and, despite ranking below Kitsune in speed, community data suggests it is actually the harder pull. Everything else in the game is a stepping stone toward these two.

For how mutations multiply a pet's stats — the fastest path to a truly endgame pet — see our [mutations guide](/mutations/all-mutations/).

## How do pets connect to rebirths?

Ride A Pet gates all seven rebirths behind specific pets: Horse → Fox → Unicorn → Phoenix → Kitsune → Kitsune → Dragon. That means you never want to sell your last Horse or Fox even after they look obsolete — the [rebirth requirements guide](/rebirths/all-rebirths/) has the full money costs alongside.

## Which pets should you ride vs. sell?

Ride your fastest available mount at all times (speed = more eggs per minute), and pen the rest for passive income. The split matters most mid-game:

- **Ride**: the highest-speed pet you own, ideally with a weather mutation — see [mutation multipliers](/mutations/all-mutations/)
- **Pen**: anything with high income/s, especially Phoenix-type earners
- **Never sell**: one of each rebirth-required pet (Horse, Fox, Unicorn, Phoenix, Kitsune ×2, Dragon)

<Callout type="tip">Hatching is volume play: commons fill nests fast, but the smart move during any 5-minute weather event is grabbing rare eggs only — a mutated Phoenix beats ten plain ones.</Callout>
