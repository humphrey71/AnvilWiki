Widely regarded as the most dominant sphere in the current meta, **Conductor Ball** brings overwhelming electrical area-of-effect damage to Roblox Ball vs Ball. Its ability to strike targets from across the arena makes it a priority pick whenever it appears in your selection pool.

## How does Conductor Ball work in arena combat?

Conductor Ball functions by charging electrical current with every wall impact and unleashing arcing lightning bolts directly into the opposing ball. Rather than relying purely on physical collisions, the lightning arcs seek out the enemy regardless of distance, inflicting massive continuous damage.

## What are the best strategies to defeat Conductor Ball?

Facing an opponent wielding Conductor Ball requires defensive counter-picking rather than attempting a pure damage race:

1. **Deploy Crowd Control**: Counter with [Shackles Ball](/balls/shackles-ball/), which binds Conductor in place and prevents it from bouncing enough times to trigger chained lightning.
2. **Utilize Flyer Family Elevation**: As explained in the [Ball Families Guide](/guides/ball-families-guide/), Flyer spheres navigate higher aerial trajectories, partially dodging the horizontal lightning arc path.
3. **Draft High Sustain**: Use [Vampire Ball](/balls/vampire-ball/) to regenerate health from early trades while setting up angle traps.
