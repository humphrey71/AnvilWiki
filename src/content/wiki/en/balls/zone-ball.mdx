## What are the most effective playstyles for Zone Ball?

To leverage Zone Ball to its fullest potential in 1v1 lobbies, apply these strategic tenets:

1. **Center-Arena Trajectories**: Aim your initial launch toward the geometric center of the arena. A centered Zone Ball maximizes the percentage of floor space covered by its radiation field.
2. **Punish Wall Huggers**: When opponents try to bank along outer walls, rebound Zone Ball into their narrow lane to trap them inside the aura.
3. **Neutralize Heavy Brawlers**: Zone Ball softens up high-durability enemies before collisions occur, ensuring your ball wins final trade margins.
