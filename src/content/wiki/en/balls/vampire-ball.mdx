Proving that rarity does not dictate victory in Roblox Ball vs Ball, **Vampire Ball** stands out as an S-Tier powerhouse despite holding a modest Rare item tier. Its unique life-draining sustain turns prolonged rounds into guaranteed attrition victories.

## How does Vampire Ball life steal work in battle?

Vampire Ball operates through dynamic vampiric absorption. Every time it makes direct contact with an enemy ball, it siphons a substantial portion of the target's HP and replenishes its own health pool. In close-quarters collision loops, this allows Vampire Ball to continually out-heal incoming damage.
